? Frequently asked

Ask the awkward stuff.

Missing something? Text us directly — we reply fast.

What space do you need to set up?+

A 10×10 ft clear area and one standard outlet. We bring everything else — lights, audio, backdrop, props.

How fast do guests get their clips?+

Instantly. Guests AirDrop / QR their clip on the spot. You get the full edited library within 48 hours.

Do you travel?+

Yes. Travel is included within 60 miles of Fort Myers. Beyond that, we add a flat per-mile rate — no surprises.

Can we brand the overlay?+

Absolutely. Send us your logo and a reference for the vibe; we'll design a loop-animated overlay that matches your event identity.

Do you have insurance?+

Fully insured up to $2M. We send certificates directly to your venue on request.

What happens if weather cancels?+

For outdoor events we pre-plan a contingency. If we cancel, your deposit rolls to a new date within 12 months at no charge.
